Product Requirements Document (PRD)
AI Tender Response Platform
1. Product Overview
1.1 Product Name
TenderAI (placeholder — you can rename later)

1.2 Product Summary
TenderAI is an end‑to‑end platform that helps businesses discover government tenders and grants, assess eligibility, and automatically generate compliant tender responses.
It uses AI to:

Analyse business profiles

Match tenders and grants

Parse tender documents

Extract requirements

Identify compliance gaps

Generate draft responses

Produce checklists and risk reports

1.3 Target Users
SMEs responding to tenders

Large enterprises with procurement teams

Consultants preparing tenders for clients

Not‑for‑profits

Startups seeking grants

1.4 Problem Statement
Businesses struggle with:

Finding relevant tenders

Understanding complex tender documents

Meeting compliance requirements

Writing high‑quality responses

Managing repetitive content

TenderAI solves this by automating 70–90% of the tender response process.
